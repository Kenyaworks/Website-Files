Our Work

Four interconnected programs that address the root causes of vulnerability in Kenyan families.

Shelter Works

Providing safe refuge for children in crisis while strengthening families through outreach, women's empowerment, and community feeding programs.

Learn more →

Education Works

Education Works opens long-term pathways out of poverty — through student sponsorship, school feeding programs, and the school infrastructure that makes learning possible.

Learn more →

MHH Works

Menstrual Health and Hygeine(MHH) education and Kenyan-made Makini reusable pads and period panties keep girls in school with dignity — while Boxers for Boys engages boys in ending stigma.

Learn more →

Community Works

Community Works trains and equips communities across Kenya to advocate for and protect the rights of their own children, women, and futures.

Learn more →
