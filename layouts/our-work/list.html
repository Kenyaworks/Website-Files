Ubuntu: I Am Because We Are

The African philosophy of Ubuntu teaches us that our humanity is bound together — when one thrives, we all thrive. Our four pillars work as one interconnected system, each strengthening the others to create lasting change.

Security - Shelter Works

We work upstream: outreach and feeding programs that keep families together and stop crises before they start. When a child does need refuge, a safe home is waiting.

→ Shelter Works provides immediate safety and family strengthening

Knowledge - Education Works

Full student sponsorship, school infrastructure, and feeding programs. No child learns hungry, or in a classroom that's falling down. We fund the whole picture, not just the fees.

→ Education Works opens pathways from classrooms to careers

Equality - Community Works

Human rights forums that train community members as advocates. They learn the law, they learn their rights, and they carry both home. The goal isn't a safe child in an unsafe place — it's a community that protects everyone in it.

→ MHH Works keeps girls in school with dignity and confidence

Empowerment - MHH Works

Period poverty pulls girls out of class every month. We provide reusable Makini pads, menstrual panties, and health education so girls stay in school — and we teach boys too, through Boxes for Boys. Dignity is something a whole generation learns together.

→ Community Works trains advocates to champion rights and justice

Our Approach

"When we lift one child, we strengthen a family. When we strengthen a family, we transform a community. When communities thrive together, we change the future."

Aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals

Our four program pillars directly advance the UN SDGs on food security, health, education, gender equality, and poverty.
