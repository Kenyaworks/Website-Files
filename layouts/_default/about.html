About Kenya Works

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to empowering vulnerable children and families in Kenya.

Our Mission

Kenya Works empowers vulnerable children and families in Kenya through holistic, dignity-centered care. We address the root causes of poverty — not just its symptoms — through security, empowerment, knowledge, and equality.

"Poverty is a denial of choices and opportunity. It is a violation of human dignity."
