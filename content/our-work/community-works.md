## Human Rights Training

We convene four-day Human Rights Forums across Kenya that bring together community members, local leaders, and youth to learn, reflect, and take action on human rights in their own communities. These immersive trainings build local capacity to prevent violence, challenge harmful norms, and defend the dignity and safety of all people

## Alternative Rites of Passage

We partner with communities to support Alternative Rites of Passage that honor culture while protecting girls from harmful practices like FGM-female genital mutilations and early marriage. By replacing cutting with education, mentorship, and public celebration, girls enter adulthood with their rights, health, and future intact.

## International and National Human Rights Days

We mobilize communities to mark key human rights observances—such as days focused on women, children, and equality—with marches, forums, school events, and community dialogues. These public celebrations keep rights at the center of community life and inspire more people to stand up for justice

## Grassroots Advocacy Networks

We equip local advocates, parents, teachers, and youth leaders with knowledge, tools, and ongoing mentorship so they can respond to rights violations and champion change where they live. These grassroots advocacy networks sustain momentum long after a forum or event ends, turning awareness into everyday action.
