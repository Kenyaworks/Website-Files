## School Feeding Programs

Hungry children cannot learn. Our school feeding programs provide nutritious meals to students across partner schools, improving nutrition, attendance, and academic performance. Consistent access to food also reduces dropout rates among vulnerable children.

## School Infrastructure

Quality education requires quality facilities. We invest in building and improving school infrastructure — from classrooms and dormitories to water systems and sanitation facilities. These improvements create safe, dignified learning environments where students can thrive.

## Kenya Works Library & Computer Lab

Our library and computer lab provide students with access to books, digital resources, and technology training. These facilities bridge the digital divide, exposing rural students to computers and internet resources that are essential for 21st-century learning.

Students gain computer literacy skills, research capabilities, and access to educational materials that expand their horizons and prepare them for higher education and careers in the digital age.

## Student Sponsorship

Our sponsorship program provides comprehensive support to vulnerable children, covering everything they need to succeed in school. Through full or partial sponsorship, we ensure no child is left behind due to poverty.

Each sponsored student receives:

- School fees & tuition
- Uniforms & shoes
- Textbooks & supplies
- Academic mentorship
- Life skills training
- Ongoing family support
