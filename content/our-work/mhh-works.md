## Makini Pad Kits

Our reusable Makini Pads kits, and period panties, provide high-quality, eco-friendly menstrual products that girls can rely on month after month. Locally made by our women-staffed production team, each kit delivers comfort and protection while creating good jobs for Kenyan women.

## Puberty & Health Education

Comprehensive puberty, menstrual health, and self-esteem education gives girls the information they need to understand their bodies and advocate for their rights. When girls have accurate, shame-free information, they are better able to stay healthy, stay in school, and stay focused on their goals.

## Boys as Allies

Through our Boxers for Boys initiative, we equip boys with both dignity-supporting underwear and accurate information about menstruation. Engaging boys as allies helps break stigma, foster respect, and build school and community environments where periods are normalized, not hidden.

## Local Production, Global Impact

Makini products are sewn in Kenya by a skilled women-led team, turning menstrual health solutions into a source of stable income for local families. This model strengthens communities, reduces waste through reusable products, and scales impact far beyond a single distribution.
