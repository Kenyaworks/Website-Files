## *Thank You!*

##

Thanks to you, Kenyans experiencing extreme vulnerability encountered life-changing opportunities.

Here are some examples of how you changed lives in 2021:

- Children went to **school**, received school **lunch** and their families benefitted from **social services**
- Communities said **no to Female Genital Mutilation** (FGM) and **child marriage**
- **Girls** were empowered to **complete secondary education** rather than becoming child brides
- Students experienced **clean water** and a new **classroom**
- Parents were trained on **positive parenting** to **eliminate violence in the home**
- Makini Pad workers enjoyed **job security**
- Girls all across Kenya received **reusable pads** and the **ability to stay in school during their periods**
- Boys all across Kenya received **boxers** and joined girls in gaining **puberty education, erasing period stigma**
- Children in crisis had a **safe place to stay**, went to bed with **full stomachs**, **love** and **t**he promise of **reuniting safely with their families**

**Thank you** for believing in **human rights for all**. Investing today creates **immediate impact** toward the **global sustainable development goals** and contributes to a more **peaceful, prosperous world for all**.

**Join us**! Keep the **momentum strong in 2022** to continue **delivering change where it is needed most**. Our team is on the ground turning your investment into action and impact for so many.
