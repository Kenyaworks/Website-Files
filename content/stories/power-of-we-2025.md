When we talk about change in Kenya, we talk about "we" — not "I." The 2025 Annual Report is a testament to what happens when donors, communities, staff, and the families we serve move together toward a shared vision.

## By the Numbers

This year, across all four of our program pillars, Kenya Works reached:

- **1,569,775 meals** provided to children and families in need
- **77,755 individuals** reached through our programs
- **61,619 shelter nights** provided to children in crisis
- **1,011 students** sponsored — 911 fully, 100 partially
- **33,000 beneficiaries** of the Makini Pad menstrual health program
- **2,080 advocates and parents** trained in human rights

## What These Numbers Mean

Numbers tell one story. But behind each shelter night is a child who slept safely. Behind each sponsored student is a family whose future changed direction. Behind each Makini Pad is a girl who stayed in class.

The Power of We is not a slogan. It is the operating principle of everything Kenya Works does. We don't deliver programs to communities — we build them with communities, and the results speak for themselves.

## Thank You

To every donor, volunteer, partner, and advocate who made 2025 possible: this is your report too. The "we" in "The Power of We" includes you.
