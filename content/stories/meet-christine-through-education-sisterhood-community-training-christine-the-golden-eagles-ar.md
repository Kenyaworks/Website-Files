## And the community is rising as well.

Christine grew up in Kuria, one of Kenya’s **most marginalized regions** — a place where **harmful traditions** and **systemic poverty** prevent many girls from attending secondary school.

She remembers the moment everything shifted. When she became a **Golden Eagle** through Kenya Works’ Transformative Power of Education initiative, she didn’t just receive a **scholarship** — she stepped into a **sisterhood**. A group of girls in Kuria who are **choosing education, choosing safety, choosing possibility**. Christine’s journey is powerful.

But she’ll be the first to tell you: **It’s not only her.** She’s part of a growing **movement** of girls who are **rising together** and **reshaping what’s possible** in their communities. Girls who, **when given the opportunity**, say **yes to education**—and **no to the harmful traditions** that once held them back. Supported by parents and leaders trained in **human rights** and **child protection**, these girls are charting new paths for themselves—*and for their communities*.

**This is what your support makes possible**: not just one girl’s success, but a ripple of change that strengthens families, empowers peer groups, and transforms entire communities.
