**A childhood protected. A future kept open.**

This is Dorothy's story, and her family's. As you read, look at how five kinds of support came together around one girl:

1. Food eased the pressure.
2. Her mother gained opportunity.
3. Dorothy gained the support she needed to stay in class.
4. Education kept her dream alive.
5. Her father saw another path.

None of these would have been enough alone. Together, they changed what came next.

## Give a Child the Whole Circle of Support

No child should have to give up her future for her family to survive.

- **$12** gives a girl a Makini Pad Dignity Kit so she can stay in school with confidence.
- **$60 a month** keeps a child learning with school fees, meals, and mentorship.
- **$350** gives a family of four daily meals for a year.
- **$4,000** funds a four-day human-rights training for 65 parents, leaders, and neighbors.
