**Come inside for a tour with Program Coordinator Caroline Gitau**

Thanks to an anonymous donor, Kenya Works bought an abandoned facility in Ongata Rongai and brought it back to life.

With support from two additional grants, we added wash and kitchen facilities, a water system and solar panels. We built with the future in mind and know the center will serve the community for years to come.

We officially opened on January 21 and can’t wait to welcome you inside for a tour!
