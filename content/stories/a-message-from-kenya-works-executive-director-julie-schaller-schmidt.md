## Shelter Works

Shelter Works ensures that vulnerable children and families have safe places to live and daily meals, basics that protect them from the most urgent risks of poverty and instability. Your support not only provides crisis shelter, but also helps fund prevention efforts within the community—strengthening families, identifying risks early, and keeping children safe from harm.

## Menstrual Health & Hygiene Works

Menstrual Health & Hygiene provides Makini Kits and education that keep girls in school with dignity and confidence. For example, your gift can supply a girl with a Makini Kit that offers about 18 months of reliable menstrual protection, dramatically reducing school absenteeism and the vulnerability that comes with missed classes.

## **Education Works**

Education Works opens the door for students like Christine through scholarships, school fees, uniforms, mentorship, school-based support, and feeding programs that keep children in class and ready to learn. Your sponsorship gift does more than pay fees; by pairing education support with school meals, it turns one investment into thousands of hours of nourished learning and a lifetime of opportunities for a student and her family.

## **Community Works**

Community Works delivers multi-day trainings on human rights and engages communities to end harmful practices like Female Genital Mutilation (FGM), child marriage, and gender-based violence. Your leadership gift can fund an entire training--empowering parents, guardians, elders, and local leaders to build safer, fairer communities that protect girls and support their education.
