Dear Friends of Kenya Works,

We're excited to share more about **Pamoja Guesthouse** a meaningful way to experience Kenya while supporting transformative community programs.

**"Pamoja" means "together" in Swahili.** When you stay at Pamoja Guesthouse, your accommodation directly funds our programs in Kenya.

Pamoja Guesthouse offers comfortable accommodation for travelers, volunteers, mission teams, and anyone who wants their visit to count for something beyond the journey itself. Guests stay in a welcoming, safe environment while connecting with the communities and programs Kenya Works serves. **It's more than lodging, it's participation.**

**100% of Pamoja Guesthouse revenue supports Kenya Works programs**
