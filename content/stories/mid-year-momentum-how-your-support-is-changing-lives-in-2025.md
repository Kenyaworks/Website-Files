**Community Needs: Why Your Support is Vital Now**

The challenges:

**Food insecurity** is on the rise

In the the last decade, Kenya has lost many of the gains it made in eradicating poverty since the start of the century.

- Poverty rates are once again on the rise, exceeding levels from a decade ago.
- The global pandemic and resulting global inflation alongside climate-related flooding and drought cycles are increasingly hurting the most vulnerable and food insecure.
- Petrol prices are driving up food costs while new taxes are decreasing household's purchasing power, especially for the most vulnerable.

Former lifelines and **aid programs** are being **defunded**

Communities served by Kenya Works face new vulnerabilities as global power structures shift, direct aid ends and development projects are abandoned.

- The abrupt and significant reduction in USAID funding has created a multifaceted crisis in Kenya, with far-reaching consequences for healthcare, humanitarian efforts and the country's overall development trajectory.
