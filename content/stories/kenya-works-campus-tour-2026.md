Kenya Works has released a video showcasing our campus and the daily activities that occur there. The video walks you through the spaces where our team and community meet, learn, plan, and take action together.

The campus tour highlights several key facilities and initiatives, including our library and crisis shelter. The video demonstrates how Kenya Works addresses multiple areas of community support:

- **Education Works** — school sponsorship, mentorship, digital literacy, and library resources
- **Menstrual Health & Hygiene Works** — dignity kit distribution and stigma-breaking outreach
- **Shelter Works** — safe housing, daily meals, and family reunification support
- **Community Works** — human rights advocacy and grassroots leadership training

We invite you to share the video with others, learn more about our programs, make a financial contribution, or explore collaborative partnerships.

Thank you for standing with Kenya Works and the communities we serve.
