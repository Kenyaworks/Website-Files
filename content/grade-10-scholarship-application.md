The Kenya Works Scholarship Programme helps students who would otherwise be unable to continue their education. Use this page to apply for **Grade 10 sponsorship**.

## How to apply

1. **Download** the application form and print it.
2. **Fill it in** using block letters. Ask the headteacher, the area chief, and a pastor, priest, imam or other leader to complete their sections, sign and stamp.
3. **Scan or photograph** the whole completed form, including any supporting documents.
4. **Upload it below**, with the contact details. The Scholarship Program Office receives it straight away.
