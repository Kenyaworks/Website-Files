## Annual Impact Reports

### 2025 Annual Report — The Power of We

- **1,569,775** meals provided across all programs
- **77,755** individuals reached
- **61,619** shelter nights provided
- **1,011** students sponsored (911 fully, 100 partially)
- **33,000** menstrual pad beneficiaries
- **2,080** advocates and parents trained
