## Our Story

Kenya Works was founded on the belief that poverty is not inevitable — it is the result of systemic barriers that can be dismantled with the right combination of shelter, education, health, and community support.

We work in partnership with Kenyan communities, not above them. Our programs are designed with input from the families and children we serve, ensuring that our work is culturally appropriate, sustainable, and genuinely needed.

## Our Approach

- **Rooted in ubuntu.** "I am because we are" — every partnership starts with shared humanity, not distance.

- **We ask "how," not "if."** Poverty looks different for every family, so we address real, specific barriers rather than one-size-fits-all fixes.

- **Four pillars, working as one.** Shelter, Health, Education, and Community Works reinforce each other for whole-life impact.

- **Community-led, always.** Solutions are shaped with the people we serve, not for them.

- **Meeting urgent needs, building lasting change.** We respond to crisis when it strikes, then walk alongside families toward stability that endures.
