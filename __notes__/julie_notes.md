## About Us

Kenya Works is a rights-based organization partnering with Kenyan communities to secure safety and dignity for vulnerable children, and the families and communities that surround them. Children are at the heart of our work — but they do not grow up in isolation. Their wellbeing depends on strong families and healthy communities, so we take a holistic approach, building the conditions in which everyone can thrive.

Our work rests on four connected pillars:

- **Shelter Works** offers safe, temporary refuge for children in crisis — and a bridge back to family. Through outreach, women's empowerment, and community feeding programs, we strengthen families so children can stay home, or return to one.

- **MHH Works** provides menstrual health, hygiene, and puberty education for both girls and boys. Our Makini reusable pads and period panties — made in Kenya, by Kenyans, for Kenyans — keep girls in school with dignity, while our Boxers for Boys program engages boys as partners in ending stigma.

- **Education Works** opens long-term pathways out of poverty — through student sponsorship, school feeding programs, and the school infrastructure that makes learning possible.

- **Community Works** trains and equips communities across Kenya to advocate for and protect the rights of their own children, women, and futures.

Lasting change comes from communities themselves — supported, not directed, by partners who listen.

## Notes for the Web Designer

- The four pillars are natural candidates to become **visual cards** on the homepage — each with an icon, the pillar name, and a short description. The paragraph version above works for the About Us page; the card version pulls double duty on the homepage as a quick-scan overview that links into deeper Programs pages.
- The mission statement uses descriptive program names (shelter, menstrual health, education, human rights training) for first-time visitors. The branded pillar names (Shelter Works, MHH Works, Education Works, Community Works) appear on the Programs page and in cards, where they have room to be explained.
- The pillar naming convention — *[Name] Works* — quietly reinforces the parent brand. Read aloud in sequence, the four pillars roll up to "Kenya Works." This is worth protecting and amplifying throughout the site.
